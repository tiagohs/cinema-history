## Full description (2736/4000)

<!-- INICIO-DESCRICAO -->
```
History of Cinema is a journey through more than 130 years of film, from the Lumière brothers and Georges Méliès to the movies and debates of today. Read at your own pace, watch hand-picked videos and discover the films, artists and movements that changed the way stories are told.

THE 8 ERAS OF CINEMA
The story is told in 8 parts, from 1895 to today. Each era brings chapters with text, images, videos, quotes and trivia:
• 1895 to 1929: the visionaries, Chaplin and Keaton, German expressionism and 1920s Hollywood
• 1930 to 1939: the talkies, Gothic horror and the first animated feature
• 1940 to 1959: film noir, musicals, Westerns, Asian masterpieces and the French New Wave
• 1960 to 1969: Brazil's Cinema Novo, the sexual revolution on screen, psychological horror and science fiction
• 1970 to 1989: auteur cinema, the first blockbusters and era-defining comedies
• 1990 to 2009: independent film, Pixar, the digital effects revolution and Latin American and European cinema
• 2010 to 2019: superheroes, a new wave of horror, animation beyond Disney and the arrival of streaming
• 2020 to today: the pandemic, the streaming wars, the Hollywood strikes, artificial intelligence and world cinema reaching the top

FILM AWARDS
Browse the major film awards the way you browse a streaming app: the Oscars (since 1929), Golden Globes, BAFTA, Critics Choice, the Actor Awards (SAG), Independent Spirit and the Cannes Film Festival. Every year, with winners and nominees in each category and the story of each ceremony.

ANIMATED TIMELINE
Cinema's milestones in chronological order, from the first public screening to recent releases.

1001 MOVIES YOU MUST SEE BEFORE YOU DIE
From classics to cult films, from horror to romance: explore the famous list organized by era and find your next movie night.

MOVIES, PEOPLE AND DIRECTORS
• A page for every film with synopsis, cast, crew and media
• Biographies and filmographies of actors, actresses and filmmakers
• Masters of Cinema: in-depth profiles of great directors

GLOSSARY AND REFERENCES
• A glossary of film terms explained in plain language
• References and suggestions: books, films, series, videos and links used to research the content

IN THREE LANGUAGES
All content is available in English, Portuguese and Spanish. Switch languages anytime in the settings.

A refreshed Material 3 design and a home screen that takes you straight to the latest chapter or back to where you left off.

Made for film students, movie lovers and anyone curious about how cinema got here.

Movie and people data provided by TMDB. This app uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Award names belong to their respective owners; this app has no official affiliation with them.
```
