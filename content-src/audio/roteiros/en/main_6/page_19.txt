The influence the nine/eleven tragedy had on American drama in the early twenty-first century.

## [01] Spike Lee  (1701 car., ~1.7 min)

In the wake of the two thousand one terrorist attacks, there were visible signs of soul-searching in American cinema. Spike Lee (born in nineteen fifty-seven) responded with the heartfelt Twenty-fifth Hour, from two thousand two. The film, which revolves around the last day of freedom of a man about to be sent to prison, is a love letter to Lee's beloved New York as well as a study of consequences and responsibility.

Dramatized reconstructions of what happened on September eleventh were dominated by Paul Greengrass's (born in nineteen fifty-five) harrowing United ninety-three, from two thousand six. Oliver Stone's (born in nineteen forty-six) World Trade Center, from two thousand six, drew criticism from all sides. Stone was also criticized for his biopic of George W. Bush, W., from two thousand eight. Although it was far from the devastating indictment some had hoped for, Stone's aim was to pose a more ambitious challenge to a system that allowed a man like him to reach the Oval Office.

The subsequent conflicts in Afghanistan and Iraq were addressed in a variety of films, but despite the presence of big stars, Syriana, from two thousand five, Good Night, and Good Luck., from two thousand five, - a clever allusion to the McCarthy witch hunt that reflected the Bush administration's "you're either with us or against us" rhetoric - Home of the Brave, from two thousand six, In the Valley of Elah, from two thousand seven, Grace Is Gone, from two thousand seven, and Stop-Loss, from two thousand eight, failed to attract a wider audience.

## [02] The Hurt Locker  (1051 car., ~1.1 min)

The Oscar-winning The Hurt Locker, from two thousand eight, could be read as an endorsement of either left- or right-wing positions on the conflict in Iraq. Ridley Scott (born in nineteen thirty-seven) contributed Body of Lies, from two thousand eight, an old-school thriller updated to explore the CIA's current dealings overseas, while David Cronenberg (born in nineteen forty-three) reminded two thousand five audiences of the homegrown "at home" depravity and the darkness lurking in everyday life in the elegant A History of Violence, from two thousand five.

Steven Spielberg's (born in nineteen forty-six) blockbusters such as Minority Report, from two thousand two, and War of the Worlds, from two thousand five, - whose apocalyptic opening scenes recall Manhattan just after the planes hit - were no longer so different from his more "serious" works. If A.I. Artificial Intelligence, from two thousand one, divided audiences and critics over its quality, the debate surrounding Munich, from two thousand five, took place in the political arena.

## [03] Tony Kushner  (1375 car., ~1.4 min)

Nevertheless, Spielberg and screenwriter Tony Kushner's (born in nineteen fifty-six) take on the Mossad agents who hunt down those responsible for the massacre at the nineteen seventy-two Olympics is powerful. In the aftermath of nine/eleven, filmmakers found themselves looking to the past, to American history, and to the roots of the national psyche. Paul Thomas Anderson (born in nineteen seventy) directed There Will Be Blood, from two thousand seven, and conveyed the message that many contemporary films explored during the Bush administration - that power corrupts and breeds paranoia and fear in those who benefit from it. With Gangs of New York, from two thousand two, and The Aviator, from two thousand four, Martin Scorsese (born in nineteen forty-two) examined the birth of the modern United States - capitalism, racial tensions, and, in the characters of the brutal Bill the Butcher and the unstable millionaire Howard Hughes, the dangers of greed and isolationism.
